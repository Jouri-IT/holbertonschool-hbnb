01 — Pavilion

Desert Pavilion

Sukoon, AlUla Desert Highlands

Residence Information

Host Omar Al-Rashid, Estate Host

Price per night $980

Set low against a dune, the Desert Pavilion opens onto its own private terrace where sand meets a poured-concrete threshold. Interiors are spare and tactile — raw plaster, linen and stone — built to hold shadow through the heat of the day and starlight after dark.

Amenities
  • In-villa WiFi
  • King Bed
  • Outdoor Rain Shower
  • Private Terrace & Daybed
