A private resort of five residences, set low in the dunes where the only sound left is your own.

The Philosophy

Stillness is the true luxury.

Sukoon — Arabic for stillness — sits low in the desert highlands of AlUla, where sound gives way to silence and the horizon becomes the only view that matters. Each residence is shaped by shadow, stone and the slow rhythm of desert light, built not to impress but to disappear into its surroundings.
